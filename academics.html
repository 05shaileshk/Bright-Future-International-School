Our Educational Structure

We design the best curriculum for children at every level, according to their needs

Primary education

In the early years, children are taught basic concepts through interactive and fun learning. The focus and on activity based learning and communication skills

  • Basic Math & language skills
  • Arts,Craft & Music classes
  • Fundamental Science & Moral Values

Middle School Curriculum

At this level,deeper knowledge of subjects begins to develop. children are encouraged to practice critical thinking, problem solving and teamwork

  • Advanced Mathematics & Science
  • Computer Applications & Coding Basics
  • Social Science & Languages

High School & Higher secondary

Advanced and focused educational structure is provided for preparation of board exams and competitive exams (JEE / NEET)

  • Science Commerce & Arts Stream
  • Career Counseling & Personality Development
  • Lab Experiments & Project Work
